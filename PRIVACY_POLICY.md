# Privacy Policy - e-Hospital SmartHealth

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Application:** e-Hospital SmartHealth Clinical AI Care Assistant  
**Organization:** PrimeCare Clinical Network & BMG5111 eHealth  

---

### 1. Information We Collect
e-Hospital SmartHealth collects and processes only the information required to provide clinical triage and healthcare telemetry synchronization:
* **Health & Telemetry Data:** Blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, body temperature, blood glucose levels, and symptoms communicated voluntarily via our WhatsApp Clinical Channel or in-app consultation assistant.
* **Contact Information:** Mobile phone number used for WhatsApp care communications, appointment booking confirmations, and care coordination with your attending physician (Dr. Patel).

### 2. How We Use Information
* To record real-time telemetry into your Electronic Health Record (EHR) chart.
* To perform automated Canadian CTAS triage evaluation and alert clinical staff for priority follow-ups.
* To facilitate appointment scheduling and medication reminders.
* We **never** sell, rent, or share personal health information with third-party advertisers or external marketing organizations.

### 3. Data Protection & Security
All clinical transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols in compliance with Canadian healthcare privacy legislation (PIPEDA / PHIPA) and HIPAA standards.

### 4. Data Deletion
Patients may request complete deletion of their communication transcripts and patient chart records at any time by contacting our clinical support desk at `support@ehospital.org`.
